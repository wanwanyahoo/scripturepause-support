Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

Local reading and study

This app does not send your reading history, notes, collections, bookmarks or preferences to the developer. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, no app account and no developer-operated sync server. Published texts and source information are stored with the identified edition. Your personal notes remain separate from published scripture and commentary.

Backup and deletion

Backups and exports are initiated by you through the system file or share interface. Keep them private; a backup can contain your personal notes. You choose the destination. The app's local data can be deleted in Settings. Deleting a collection does not delete its notes. Removing a purchase does not delete existing notes. Apple's device backup and retention settings remain under your control.

Purchases

Optional purchases use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment, account and refund information. The app reads verified purchase access; the developer does not receive your Apple account credentials or card details.

Publisher websites and support

Opening a published source loads its identified website, subject to the publisher's privacy policy. Online works require internet. These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access information under GitHub's privacy statement. No additional analytics, tracking pixels or third-party fonts are added. Support issues on GitHub are public; do not post private notes, credentials or receipts.
