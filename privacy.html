Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026. This policy covers ScripturePause: Bible Blocker, also named Still in the app (org.selah.still).

Information kept on your device

The app stores reading history, bookmarks, preferences, private notes, quiet intentions and reflections locally. It has no app account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated synchronization server. The developer does not receive your notes, selected apps or browsing history through the app.

Apple Screen Time manages your app selections through opaque tokens. Shared app-group storage lets the app, its protection extensions and widgets coordinate on your device. Optional web filtering is performed by iOS. Authorization is requested through Apple's system interface and can be revoked in iOS Settings.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes optional Still Plus purchases through StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified transaction information to provide or revoke paid access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Restore with the same Apple account; Apple's policies apply to payment, purchase history, refunds and system services.

The app may request permission for local reminders. You can change notification settings in iOS Settings. This app does not send push notifications through a developer server.

Backups, sharing and deletion

Backup files are created only when you choose to export. They contain your reading records, notes, bookmarks and quiet reflections; handle them as private documents. Export does not include Screen Time authorization, app-selection tokens, an active quiet window or purchase ownership. The destination you choose, such as Files or a cloud provider, controls any resulting transfer and storage.

You can delete notes and quiet reflections in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data subject to iOS backup and shared-container behavior. Delete exported copies and device/cloud backups separately if desired. Apple's device backup and retention settings remain under your control.

External links and support

Scripture source, music attribution, Apple license and support links may open third-party websites. Their own privacy policies apply. These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical access information under the GitHub privacy statement. These pages contain no added analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking pixels.

GitHub support issues are public. Do not post personal notes, credentials, app selections, purchase receipts or other private information. Information you voluntarily submit in a support request is used to understand and respond to that request. Contact support.

Changes

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date. This policy does not replace Apple's Screen Time, StoreKit, iOS or App Store privacy information.
