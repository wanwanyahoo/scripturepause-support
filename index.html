A little room for what matters.

ScripturePause is the App Store name for Still, a private Bible reading and focus app. Set aside time for rest, presence, prayer or meaningful work, then reflect and try again with your own adjustment.

App protection and quiet windows

Allow Screen Time in Plan, then select the apps you want to protect. Quiet windows last 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Reading is optional and does not end quiet protection. You can always end early for free from Today or Plan. Daily read-to-unlock rules are independent; ending a quiet window does not cancel an overlapping daily rule.

Without authorization or selected apps, the app clearly offers a personal timer, which does not add system restrictions. If you need access, end the quiet window and pause or turn off any active daily rule in Plan. You can revoke authorization in iOS Settings.

Reading, languages and music

Nineteen interface languages and nineteen source-identified Bible editions are included. Not every interface language has a matching complete Bible. Scripture is retained from its original published edition, never machine-translated. Open the edition information for its source and attribution.

Music is optional and starts only when you choose a track. Adjust volume, pause, stop or set a stop timer in Reading music. Playback stops when the app leaves the foreground. There is no synthetic narration.

Purchases and backups

One complete daily rule and quiet windows are free. Still Plus is an optional one-time purchase for up to eight daily rules and optional web filtering during daily focus. Restore purchases with the Apple account originally used to purchase. Refunds are managed by Apple; they remove paid access while keeping your local data and free rule.

Export a backup before deleting the app or replacing your phone. Backups include your readings, notes, bookmarks and quiet reflections. They do not transfer Screen Time authorization, selected-app tokens, an active quiet window or Plus ownership. Choose protected storage for exported files; they contain your private writing.

Get support

Open a support request. Include the app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. This is a public GitHub issue: do not include your personal notes, Apple credentials, app selections, purchase receipts or other private information. The app does not require a GitHub account.
